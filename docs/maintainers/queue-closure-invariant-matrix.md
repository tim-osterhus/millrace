# Queue Closure Invariant Matrix

Queue closure is an audited operator transition over selected workflow state.
It reuses `CloseWorkItem`; it does not delete queue records, overload
quarantine interventions, or signal runner adapters.

| Authority field/record | Legal state/sequence | Illegal/corrupt state | Compiler rule | Admission/runtime rule | Durable/projection rule | Test proof |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Target identity and kind | One open work item closes with `queue cancel`; one complete lineage closes with `queue cancel-lineage`. | Absent work or lineage, already-closed work, an unsupported durable target kind, or target/audit membership drift. | N/A: targets are current operator/runtime state, not compiler-authored declarations. | Refuse `queued_work_not_found`, `queued_lineage_not_found`, or `queued_work_not_cancelable`; accepted input produces one `QueueClosureRecord`. | Versioned-row decoding refuses unsupported kinds; relations refuse target and membership drift without writing bytes. | `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_closes_one_queued_work_item_and_replays`; `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_lineage_uses_selected_plan_and_stable_success_code`; `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_returns_stable_refusal_codes`; `tests/substrate/test_persistence_integrity_refusals.py::test_corrupt_queue_closure_row_refuses_without_mutation` |
| Selected-plan pin through apply | Every member and queue audit retain the exact selected `PlanRef` observed at decision. | Supplied fingerprint is not selected, a member belongs to another plan, or selected plan changes after decision. | N/A: compiled plans establish immutable identities; this command selects current state. | Refuse `selected_plan_mismatch`, `queued_work_plan_mismatch`, or `queued_lineage_plan_mismatch`; stale apply raises `StateConcurrencyError` before audit or close mutations. | Load validates the audit pin and every member pin. | `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_apply_refuses_a_stale_selected_plan_and_replays`; `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_refusals_preserve_queue_closure_state`; `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_returns_stable_refusal_codes`; `tests/substrate/test_persistence_integrity_refusals.py::test_corrupt_queue_closure_row_refuses_without_mutation` |
| Complete lineage membership | All current work items in the selected lineage close together. | A member is closed, belongs to another plan, or appears after decision. | N/A: lineage membership is authoritative runtime state. | Whole-set preflight and member generation, plan, open-state, and lineage expectations fence apply. | Audit work, activation, and run IDs equal authoritative membership. | `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_lineage_closure_preflights_and_closes_complete_membership`; `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_lineage_closure_refuses_partial_or_wrong_plan_membership`; `tests/substrate/test_persistence_atomicity.py::test_queue_lineage_apply_refusal_leaves_durable_bytes_unchanged` |
| Claim and runner-session safety | Unclaimed queued work closes; a clean terminal session can be explicitly closed after reload. | Claim races; created, starting, running, cancellation-requested, terminating, and orphan-risk sessions; lost sessions. | N/A: a terminal `pending`/`orphan_risk` cleanup pairing is invalid at the typed-record boundary. Nonterminal pending or orphan-risk sessions use the documented live refusal; lost sessions use the lost refusal. | Refuse `queued_work_claimed`, `queued_runner_session_live`, `queued_runner_session_lost`, or `queued_runner_cleanup_unresolved` when representable. Activation, run, and session snapshots fence apply. | Load refuses accepted closures that retain unsafe sessions or unresolved claims. | `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_fences_claim_races`; `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_refuses_every_unsafe_session_aftermath` |
| Existing close-work machinery | Accepted closure creates one `ClosedWorkItemRecord` per member with `close_kind=queue_cancellation`. | Close rows have runner, action, or intervention provenance, or disagree with the audit. | N/A: `CloseWorkItem` is kernel mutation authority. | The decision emits `RecordQueueClosure` plus existing `CloseWorkItem` mutations. | Relations require a matching queue-owned close record and reject extraneous provenance. | `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_closes_one_queued_work_item_and_replays`; `tests/substrate/test_persistence_integrity_refusals.py::test_corrupt_queue_closure_row_refuses_without_mutation`; `tests/substrate/test_persistence_atomicity.py::test_queue_closure_commit_failure_rolls_back_audit_and_all_closes` |
| Durable input identity and replay | Repeating an accepted input returns the original result without mutation, including after a later default-plan change. | The same input ID changes target, plan, actor, or reason. | N/A: shared input-idempotency contract. | Matching receipts replay; changed payloads refuse `idempotency_conflict`. | Receipts, transition, governance event, trace, audit, and close rows do not duplicate. | `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_apply_refuses_a_stale_selected_plan_and_replays`; `tests/kernel/test_queue_closure.py::test_queue_closure_refusals_preserve_queue_closure_state`; `tests/cli/test_cli_queue_commands.py::test_queue_cancel_returns_stable_refusal_codes` |
| Bounded observation | Status, trace, and doctor expose queue closures. | More than 20 records or more than 20 work, activation, or run IDs would make the projection unbounded or silently lose totals. | N/A: read-only projection. | Projection retains 20 records and 20 IDs per kind while reporting exact total and omitted counts. | Audit rows remain authoritative; truncation is explicit and deterministic. | `tests/cli/test_cli_status_commands.py::test_queue_closure_projections_bound_records_and_identity_lists` constructs one lineage closure with 21 work, activation, and clean-terminal run identities, then proves the 20-item cap and one omitted identity on all three JSON surfaces. |
